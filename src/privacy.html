Privacy Policy

Ring Twist · last updated /*__BUILT__*/

This game is free, has no accounts, and never asks for your name, e-mail address or any other personal detail.

Saved on your own device

Your progress is kept in your browser's local storage under the key rotatering.save.v1: the level you have reached, your best scores, your fireworks and hammers, which tips you have seen, and the sound and vibration switches. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

Cloud save

When the game is played on CrazyGames, that same saved progress is also handed to the CrazyGames SDK so your levels follow you between devices, and the rewarded videos you choose to watch are served by CrazyGames. Both are governed by CrazyGames' own privacy policy. On any other site neither happens.

What is sent to us

Nothing. This version of the game sends no analytics, statistics or identifiers anywhere.
